THE PROPHECY

12.18.26

IS DOOMSDAY

Marvel Studios' Avengers: Doomsday arrives in cinemas — and Bennett's own court convenes before the throne is claimed.

"You call it tyranny. I call it order. Every empire before mine was built on chaos disguised as freedom — mine will be built on code, and it will not fail." — issued from the throne of Latveria

Doom Protocol is Bennett University's tribute to Marvel's most dangerous mind — Victor von Doom: monarch, scientist, sorcerer, and the man about to become the MCU's greatest threat this December in Avengers: Doomsday.

Hosted by the GeeksforGeeks Student Chapter, this is not a hackathon dressed in a costume. It's a two-day court where builders, hackers and problem-solvers compete for a place among the Sovereign's chosen — through code, logic, and a little bit of showmanship.

No capes required. A working prototype and a strong argument will do.

THE TRIALS

Four paths into the court

Pick your trial when you register — each runs its own judging, its own rewards, and its own chance to catch the Sovereign's eye.

TRIAL I

Circuits of Iron

A 24-hour build sprint. Ship a working product from problem statements inspired by Latverian "governance tech" — real-world, slightly villainous framing included.

Team hackathon
TRIAL II

Trial of Wit

Timed competitive programming rounds. Three levels of difficulty, one leaderboard, zero mercy from the judges.

Solo · DSA
TRIAL III

Court of Illusion

Workshops and talks on AI, systems design and security — taught by engineers who, frankly, explain things better than any sorcerer.

Talks & workshops
TRIAL IV

Masks & Machines

Fan art, cosplay and a Marvel-lore quiz for anyone who'd rather build a legend than a login page.

Open · Casual
